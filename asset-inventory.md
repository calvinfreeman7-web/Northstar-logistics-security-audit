# Asset Inventory

## Purpose

This asset inventory identifies the critical technology and information assets used by NorthStar Logistics. Each asset is evaluated based on its function, security concerns, and importance to business operations.

| Asset | Category | Security Concern | Criticality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee Laptops | Endpoint | Malware, unauthorized access, data theft | High |
| Company Email | Cloud Service | Phishing, credential theft, account compromise | High |
| Transportation Management System (TMS) | Business Application | Unauthorized access, operational disruption | Critical |
| Customer Database | Data | Data breach, unauthorized disclosure | Critical |
| Office Wi-Fi Network | Network | Unauthorized network access, interception | High |
| Cloud File Storage | Cloud/Data | Data leakage, excessive permissions | High |
| Employee Smartphones | Mobile Endpoint | Lost devices, compromised accounts | Medium |
| Backup System | Recovery | Ransomware, failed or inaccessible backups | Critical |

## Key Findings

The Transportation Management System, customer database, and backup system are considered critical assets because compromise or loss of these systems could significantly disrupt business operations.

Company email and employee laptops also represent high-risk assets because they provide potential entry points for phishing, malware, and credential-based attacks.
