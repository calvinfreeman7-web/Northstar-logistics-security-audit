# Executive Summary

## Overview

NorthStar Logistics is a simulated transportation and logistics organization with approximately 50 employees. A cybersecurity risk assessment was conducted to identify critical assets, evaluate cybersecurity risks, recommend security controls, and align security activities with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0.

## Key Findings

The assessment identified five primary cybersecurity risks:

| Risk | Risk Score | Rating |
|---|---:|---|
| Ransomware | 25 | Critical |
| Phishing / Credential Theft | 16 | High |
| Unauthorized Access / Excessive Permissions | 12 | High |
| Data Loss / Backup Failure | 8 | Medium |
| Insecure Wi-Fi / Network Access | 6 | Medium |

Ransomware represents the organization's highest-priority cybersecurity risk due to its potential to disrupt critical systems, encrypt business data, and interrupt logistics operations.

Phishing and credential theft also present significant risk because compromised employee accounts could provide unauthorized access to organizational systems and information.

## Recommended Actions

NorthStar Logistics should prioritize the following actions:

1. Deploy endpoint detection and response (EDR) and centrally managed anti-malware.
2. Require multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all company accounts.
3. Implement role-based access control (RBAC) and conduct regular access reviews.
4. Implement automated backups and routinely test restoration procedures.
5. Strengthen wireless security using WPA3 and separate guest Wi-Fi from the corporate network.

## Remediation Priority

Based on the risk assessment, ransomware mitigation should receive the highest priority, followed by phishing and credential protection and improvements to access control.

Medium-rated risks should be addressed after the organization's Critical and High risks have been reduced to acceptable levels.

## NIST CSF Alignment

The recommended security improvements align with the six NIST Cybersecurity Framework functions:

- Govern
- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

Using these functions provides NorthStar Logistics with a structured approach for managing cybersecurity risk and improving organizational resilience.

## Conclusion

This assessment identified cybersecurity weaknesses that could affect NorthStar Logistics' systems, information, and business operations.

Implementing the recommended controls will reduce exposure to identified threats, strengthen access security, improve threat detection, and increase the organization's ability to respond to and recover from cybersecurity incidents.
