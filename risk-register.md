# Cybersecurity Risk Register

## Risk Assessment Methodology

NorthStar Logistics uses a 5x5 risk assessment methodology.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

### Rating Scale

- Likelihood: 1 (Rare) to 5 (Almost Certain)
- Impact: 1 (Minimal) to 5 (Severe)
- Low Risk: 1–4
- Medium Risk: 5–9
- High Risk: 10–16
- Critical Risk: 17–25

## Risk Register

| Risk | Affected Asset | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Score | Rating |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Phishing / Credential Theft | Company Email | 4 | 4 | 16 | High |
| Ransomware | Employee Laptops / TMS / Backup System | 5 | 5 | 25 | Critical |
| Unauthorized Access / Excessive Permissions | Cloud File Storage / TMS | 3 | 4 | 12 | High |
| Insecure Wi-Fi / Network Access | Office Wi-Fi Network | 2 | 3 | 6 | Medium |
| Data Loss / Backup Failure | Backup System / Customer Database | 2 | 4 | 8 | Medium |
